2. Newsroom Roles & Production Workflow

CTE Standard CTB.02.01
  • Talent & Editorial: Anchors and reporters research stories, write script copy, conduct field interviews, and verify facts.
  • Control Room Crew: Technical Director operates video switchers; Audio Engineer manages mix boards; Graphics Specialist runs lower-thirds.
  • Floor Crew: Camera Operators execute framing/focus; Floor Manager coordinates timing cues with talent.
💡 Crew Spotlight: Cross-Communication Protocol

"The Technical Director and Floor Manager must communicate via headset tally cues 5 seconds prior to going live. Clear audio cues prevent missed camera cuts!"

3. Journalistic Integrity & News Ethics

CTE Standard CTB.01.02
  • SPJ Code Alignment: Seek truth and report it; minimize harm; act independently; be accountable and transparent.
  • Source Verification: Always cross-reference claims with minimum two reliable sources prior to air.
  • Zero Tolerance: Fabrication of quotes, staging non-feature events, or editorializing hard news triggers immediate crew suspension.
⚖️ Ethical Scenario Challenge

"If an eyewitness interview contradicts official school statements, air both perspectives with clear source attribution rather than taking a side."

4. Campus Reporting & Public Law

CTE Standard CTB.01.02
  • Public vs. Restricted Space: Filming in hallways or outdoor commons follows general public guidelines; active classrooms require instructor permission.
  • Minimizing Disruption: Field crews must not obstruct instruction, block egress routes, or breach emergency protocols.
  • Interview Courtesy: State your full name, outlet, and broadcast intent before pressing record.
🏫 Field Access Rule of Thumb

"Never place tripods or light stands within 6 feet of emergency exit doors or main stairwells during passing periods!"

5. Copyright, Media Releases & Privacy

CTE Standard CTB.01.04
  • Intellectual Property: Broadcasts must use royalty-free music, Creative Commons assets, or student-composed audio tracks.
  • Fair Use Criteria: Short commentary/criticism excerpts are allowed, but commercial music overlays are strictly prohibited.
  • Media Clearance: All featured interview subjects must have signed media releases on file in the main station drive.
🎵 Copyright Pro-Tip: Audio Library Access

"Always source intro/outro music from the station's pre-cleared YouTube Audio Library folder. Commercial tracks will get our online stream muted instantly!"

6. Lab Safety & Asset Management

CTE Standard CTB.05.01
  • Resource Stewardship: Broadcast cameras and production gear represent significant school capital investments.
  • Gear Privilege Policy: Access to field kits is contingent on passing this safety orientation and maintaining clean check-out records.
  • Zero Hazard Policy: Keep food, sugary beverages, and unsealed liquids completely clear of editing suites and control consoles.
⚠️ Lab Safety Reminder

"Keep all drinks away from editing desks! Any unsealed liquids near computers will receive a warning and must be moved to designated storage tables immediately."

7. Rigging Cameras & Tripod Protocol

CTE Standard CTB.01.03
  • Lock Verification: Always engage pan and tilt drag locks BEFORE mounting or unmounting camera quick-release plates.
  • Three-Point Contact: Keep hands secured on camera body and tripod handle while adjusting column height.
  • Transport Protocol: Never move a tripod with the camera attached; unmount the body into its padded case before moving locations.
🔍 3-Point Pre-Flight Tripod Check

"1. Lock pan drag. 2. Verify base plate click. 3. Double-check thumb lock screw BEFORE letting go of the camera body!"

8. Studio Lighting & Electrical Safety

CTE Standard CTB.05.01
  • Thermal Hazards: Studio key lights and halogen fixtures generate intense heat; handle housings with heat-resistant gloves only.
  • Trip Hazards: Secure all floor-run AC cables and XLR audio lines with heavy gaffer tape across walking paths.
  • Cable Coiling: Use the over-under coiling method for all cables to prevent wire fraying and structural cable memory damage.
🔥 Thermal Hazard Protocol

"Allow studio lights 10 minutes of fan-cooled cooldown time before packing them into storage bags. Melted canvas cases are non-repairable!"

9. Daktronics Board Interface Operations

CTE Standard CTB.03.01
  • System Access: Log in using authorized Chisholm Station crew credentials only; unauthorized setting edits are logged.
  • Graphic Specs: Render all stadium display graphics at native resolution (16:9 aspect ratio, uncompressed PNG/MP4 format).
  • Live Feed Overlays: Coordinate scorekeeping triggers with production directors during active athletic events.
🎮 Stadium Display Graphic Standard

"Keep all graphic text inside the 80% Action-Safe zone to prevent stadium border bezels from cutting off player names!"

10. Daktronics System Shutdown

CTE Standard CTB.03.02
  • Order of Operations: 1) Close display software, 2) Send power-off signal to LED wall receivers, 3) Power down console.
  • Data Backup: Export session logs and archive event graphic packages to the main server before logging off.
  • Console Cover Protocol: Place dust covers over Daktronics control terminals after components have cooled.
🔌 Shutdown Sequence Memory Lock

"Software Exit ➡️ Video Board Receiver Power-Off ➡️ Console Power Switch ➡️ Dust Cover Application."

11. Equipment Damage & Fault Logging

CTE Standard CTB.05.01
  • Pre/Post Inspection: Inspect lenses for smudges/scratches and cables for tears during checkout and checkin.
  • Immediate Logging: If gear malfunctions or sustains damage, submit a station Incident Form immediately—do not store broken gear back on shelves.
  • Honesty Policy: Promptly reporting accidental damage maintains crew standing; concealing damage leads to revocation of checkout privileges.
📋 Incident Logging Quick-Action

"Spotted a frayed XLR cable or damaged tripod latch? Tag it with red tape and give to Mr. Boehm immediately."
