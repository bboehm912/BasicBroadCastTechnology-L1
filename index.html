Slide 1: Course Orientation & Overview

  • Welcome to Broadcast Journalism & Media Production!
  • Our goal: Create accurate, professional broadcasts for Chisholm High School.
  • Focus of Week 1: Newsroom conduct, ethics, and studio equipment safety protocols.

Slide 2: Newsroom Roles & Station Crew

  • Anchors & Field Reporters: Deliver factual news and conduct ethical interviews.
  • Technical Director & Camera Ops: Manage broadcast hardware and camera setups.
  • Station Manager: Oversees equipment checkouts, safety, and schedules.

Slide 3: Journalistic Responsibility (Standard 1)

  • Maintain strict factual accuracy in all reporting.
  • Verify sources prior to broadcasting any story segment.
  • Avoid intentional distortion, bias, or sensationalism.

Slide 4: Field Conduct & On-Campus Filming

  • Always represent Chisholm High School professionally in public spaces.
  • Respect campus policies regarding filming locations and class disruptions.
  • Obtain permission from instructors prior to filming inside active classrooms.

Slide 5: Privacy Releases & Consent Protocols

  • All interview subjects must sign or agree to student media release protocols.
  • Never broadcast identifiable footage of individuals who decline to be recorded.
  • Safeguard digital media files on authorized station drives only.

Slide 6: Equipment Safety Fundamentals (Standard 2)

  • Lab equipment is costly and vital to our daily station broadcasts.
  • Treat every camera, lens, tripod, and cable with care and respect.
  • Equipment checkout privileges depend on 100% compliance with safety rules.

Slide 7: Camera & Tripod Operation Rules

  • Always verify pan/tilt locks are engaged before mounting or unmounting cameras.
  • Maintain 3-point contact whenever transporting equipment kits.
  • Use neck/shoulder straps during all mobile and field shooting sessions.

Slide 8: Studio Lighting & Power Cable Safety

  • Never touch active studio lighting fixtures without protective heat gloves.
  • Gaff-tape power cables across walkways to eliminate trip hazards.
  • Never force stuck power connectors or force tight lens mounts.

Slide 9: Daktronics Control Board Interface

  • Access the Daktronics control interface using assigned station credentials only.
  • Do not modify system settings outside approved broadcast operational guidelines.
  • Ensure control station hardware is kept clear of all food and liquids.

Slide 10: System Shutdown Sequences

  • Always follow standard shutdown procedures for Daktronics and studio consoles.
  • Power down monitors, switcher units, and wireless audio receivers in order.
  • Return all cables neatly coiled to assigned storage racks.

Slide 11: Incident & Hazard Reporting

  • Inspect all gear thoroughly during checkout and checkin.
  • Report damaged cables, faulty LED modules, or malfunctioning gear immediately.
  • Reporting issues immediately protects station privileges for everyone.
